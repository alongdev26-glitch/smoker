Privacy Policy — Quitly

Last updated: September 2026

Quitly ("the app", "we") helps you track and reduce smoking, vaping, or cannabis use. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information — if you choose to create an account, we collect your email address and password (handled entirely by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password ourselves), or basic profile info (name, email) if you sign in with Google.
  • Profile & onboarding answers — the name, birth year, gender, substance type, and habits you enter when setting up the app, used only to personalize your plan.
  • Usage data you log — entries you record in the app (date/time, quantity, type, trigger), your program settings, and your streak/visit history.
  • Coach chat messages — if you use the in-app "Coach" craving-support chat, your messages are sent securely to Anthropic's Claude AI service to generate a supportive reply. Messages are not used to identify you beyond an anonymous rate-limit counter.

How your information is stored

By default, all your data stays only on your device (local storage). If you create an account (email/password or Google), your data is additionally synced to Google Firebase (Firestore) so you can access it from other devices. This sync only happens for signed-in accounts.

Third-party services we use

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, Firestore database, Hosting, and Cloud Functions. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic — powers the optional in-app Coach chat feature. Governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your choices

  • You can use the app fully without creating an account — your data then never leaves your device.
  • You can sign out at any time from the More tab.
  • You can request deletion of your account and synced data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Children's privacy

Quitly is intended for adults looking to quit or reduce smoking, vaping, or cannabis use, and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
